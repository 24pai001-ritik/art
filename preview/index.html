The artist

Jomy Johnson

Jomy Johnson is a fine artist in Ahmedabad. She paints portraits, still lifes and flowers, in oil on linen and paper and in watercolour, and draws in coloured pencil, charcoal and graphite.

Painting approach
Out of the darkHer oils begin on a deep dark ground. One pale form is brought up out of the shadow, and the rest is left quiet.
From lifeRoses and hibiscus are painted in watercolour straight from life, looking at the real flower.
Drawing firstShe copies the classical Charles Bargue plates in charcoal and graphite, so form and light are right before colour.
